Explore Ayurveda records

Select Ayurveda as the source medical system when adding a record or searching for a documented term. The search uses official Standardized Ayurvedic Terminology (SAT) via the Ministry of Ayush NAMASTE portal.

Ayurveda NAMASTE SAT Tridosha Pathology Record comparison

Use verified information carefully

Ayurveda records use systemic and holistic terminology (Dosha, Dhatu, Agni) that differs fundamentally from modern molecular diagnostic criteria. Review the original record and consult a qualified healthcare professional.

About

About MedVeda

MedVeda helps users organize, understand, and compare information recorded across Allopathy, Ayurveda, and Homeopathy.

Important Medical Safety Disclaimer: This prototype is designed exclusively for medical terminology comparison, educational, and research purposes. Textual or conceptual similarities across different medical systems do NOT establish clinical equivalence. This prototype does not diagnose diseases, recommend treatments, or replace advice from qualified healthcare professionals.

Important: Text similarities do not establish clinical equivalence. This prototype does not diagnose diseases or recommend medical treatments.

Official Medical Knowledge Sources Integrated

  • Allopathy: SNOMED CT India Release via NRCeS Bharat Health Terminology Service (BHTS FHIR R4).
  • Ayurveda: Standardized Ayurvedic Terminology (SAT) via Ministry of Ayush NAMASTE Portal.
  • Homeopathy: Disease Terminology (ICD-10) via NAMASTE and Pharmacopoeial Medicine Monographs via PCIM&H (HPI Volumes I–XI).
